    About the image converter

    Choose PNG, JPG or WebP as the output format and drop in your images. You can set a maximum width and, for JPG, the quality. Pictures with transparency get a background color of your choice when saved as JPG, because JPG cannot store transparency.

    PNG is best for graphics and screenshots, JPG for photos, and WebP for smaller files on websites.
