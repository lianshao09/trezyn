How it works

Every tool on this site runs inside your browser. Your files, text and passwords are processed on your device and are not sent to a server. You do not have to take our word for it, and this page shows how to check.

What stays on your device

Photos, PDFs, Office files, text to encrypt, passwords and QR pictures or camera frames are opened and changed by JavaScript that runs in your own browser. The result is saved as a download from your own memory. The site has no upload form and no server code that could receive a file.

The libraries that do the work, such as the PDF and QR code libraries, are stored on this site and load from it, not from another company.

How to check it yourself

  1. 1. Open any tool and press F12 (or right-click and choose Inspect), then open the Network tab.
  2. 2. Use the tool with a file. You will see no request that carries your file or text. Only the page, its scripts, its styles and its fonts appear, all from this site.
  3. 3. Load a tool page, switch your device to airplane mode, and use it again. It keeps working, because nothing needs the internet.
  4. 4. The server also tells your browser to block connections to other sites. Look for the Content-Security-Policy header in the first request.

Closed room and Direct share

These two tools are separate sites, closedroom.site and directshare.site, and they work differently from everything above. They connect two browsers directly (WebRTC), so messages and files travel between you and the other person without a server in the middle. To find each other, the browsers contact public relay servers, which only see a hash of the room name and technical connection details. The other person can see your IP address, and on strict networks the connection can fail because there is no relay for the data itself. Nothing is stored.

Open source

The whole site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript under the MIT license. What you see with View Source is what runs, and the same files are published as a repository, so anyone can read them, copy them or run the site themselves.

What does leave your browser

Loading the pages: your browser asks this site for them, so the hosting company, like any host, may keep ordinary server logs with the address, time and page requested. We use no analytics, no cookies and no advertising scripts, and fonts are served from this site, not from Google.

The Privacy check page: only when you press its network button, it asks api.ipify.org for your public IP address and uses a public STUN server to test what WebRTC reveals. This is the point of that tool, and nothing happens until you press the button.

What is saved in your browser

Only your theme choice and the last settings of dropdowns, sliders, colors and tick boxes, such as image quality. They are stored in local storage on your device. Text, passwords, files and encryption keys are never saved. Clearing the site data in your browser removes it all.

After your first visit the site also keeps its own pages, scripts and icons in your browser (a service worker) so that it opens without a connection and can be installed like an app. It only handles files from this site, never what you type or open. Pages are fetched fresh when you are online, so updates arrive normally.

Limits you should know

A browser extension can read the pages you visit, so use a clean profile for very sensitive work. If you forget an encryption password, the data cannot be recovered. Removing metadata does not remove what a photo or document shows. Redaction should be tested on the result. See the guides for each task.
