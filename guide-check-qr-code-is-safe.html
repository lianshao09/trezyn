How to Check a QR Code Before You Open It

You cannot tell by looking at a QR code where it leads. Criminals stick fake codes over real ones on parking meters, posters and menus, or send them in emails, because people open the link without thinking.

Steps

  1. Read the code without opening it. Use the QR reader and give it a picture or let it use your camera. It shows the content as text and does not open it.
  2. Look at the domain. Read the part of the address before the first single slash. A bank link should end with the real bank domain, not a longer name that only contains it.
  3. Be careful with short links. A shortened link hides its target. If you do not trust the source, do not open it.
  4. Decide. If the code asks you to log in, pay or install something, go to the official site by typing its address yourself.

Good to know

A code that sits on a sticker over another one is a warning sign. In public places, check that it has not been stuck on top.

Payment codes and Wi-Fi codes do more than open a page. Make sure you know who made them.

The reader works inside your browser, and the picture or camera frame is not sent anywhere.
