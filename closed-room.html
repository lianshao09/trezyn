Closed room

A chat room that exists only while you and your friend have it open. You both type the same secret phrase and your browsers connect to each other directly. When you close the tab, the conversation is gone.

Open Closed room ↗

How to use it

  1. 1. Agree on a room phrase with the other person, ideally in another channel, and make it long and hard to guess.
  2. 2. Both of you open Closed room, type a nickname (up to 24 characters) and the same room phrase. Upper and lower case matter.
  3. 3. Wait a moment for the two browsers to find each other, then write in the message box. Press Leave or close the tab to end the chat.

How it works

The two browsers connect directly with WebRTC, so messages go from one to the other without passing through a server. To find each other they contact public relay servers. The relays receive only a hash of the phrase, not the phrase itself, plus the technical details needed to connect.

Nothing is saved: no account, no history and no log of the conversation on a server.

Good to know

The other person can see your IP address, as in any direct connection.

There is no relay for the messages themselves, so on very strict networks (some company or mobile networks) the connection can fail. Try another network if it does.

Anyone who guesses the phrase can join the room, so use a long phrase that is not obvious. Messages and the phrase are not recoverable if you lose them.
