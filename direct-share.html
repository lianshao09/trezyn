Direct share

Send files directly from your browser to someone else's. The file never goes to a server and is not stored anywhere. The channel closes by itself after 60 minutes.

Open Direct share ↗

How to use it

  1. 1. Open Direct share. Press Generate random ID, or type an ID of your own, then press Create channel.
  2. 2. Press Copy invite link and send it to the other person, or send them the ID.
  3. 3. They open the link, or enter the ID and press Join channel. When the two browsers are connected, choose the file and send it.

How it works

The two browsers connect directly with WebRTC. Public relays only introduce them to each other. They do not carry the file, and the file does not pass through a server.

Nothing is stored and there are no accounts. The channel closes after 60 minutes, and you can close the tab sooner.

Good to know

Both people must keep their pages open while the file is sent.

The other person can see your IP address, as in any direct connection.

There is no relay for the data, so on very strict networks the connection can fail. Try another network if it does.

Send the link only to the person you want to share with. Whoever has it can join the channel while it is open.
