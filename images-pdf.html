      About images and PDF conversion

      Images to PDF puts your photos or scans into one PDF, one image per page. You can reorder them, choose A4, Letter or fit-to-image page size, the margin and the image quality.

      PDF to images turns each page into a PNG or JPG at 72 to 300 dpi. Use 150 dpi for general use and 300 dpi for printing. Several pages are offered as a single ZIP download.

      Both tools run in your browser, so documents and photos stay private.
